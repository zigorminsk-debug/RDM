The submodule is pinned to upstream commit `87baff6c80e16087c146937bca81d2bdb788e236` of [FreeRDP/FreeRDP](https://github.com/FreeRDP/FreeRDP). Its upstream `LICENSE`, source-file notices, and component-specific license terms are retained in the submodule. The upstream repository includes Apache-2.0 and MPL-2.0 licensed components; consult those notices when redistributing the application.

RDM's own connection-management code is separate from the FreeRDP source. No FreeRDP source files have been copied into this project.
